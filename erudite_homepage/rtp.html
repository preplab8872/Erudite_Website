LEARNING IN REAL LIFE

Communication is a skill you can practise.

Recruitment preparation works best when candidates rehearse aptitude, problem solving, interviews and how they explain their thinking.

WHAT THE PROCESS CAN INCLUDE

Recruitment is a sequence of assessments.

The details depend on the employer and role. Erudite’s RTP page describes these common stages and areas of preparation.

01

Aptitude tests

Quantitative ability, data interpretation, verbal ability, reading comprehension, logical reasoning and general awareness.

02

Technical tests

Subject knowledge in the student's discipline, which can include programming, electronics or other technical topics according to the role.

03

Group discussions

Practice listening, contributing relevant points, communication, collaboration and decision-making.

04

Personal interviews

Prepare for technical and HR discussions, clarity of goals, strengths, experience and communication.
