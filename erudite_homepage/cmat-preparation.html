01 / CMAT PREPARATION

A stronger answer starts with a clearer method.

Erudite’s CMAT course description places concept refreshers, strategy discussions, practice tests and detailed review alongside general-awareness learning and interview preparation.

COURSE OVERVIEW

Know what to work on.

For applicants considering management programmes that accept CMAT scores. These focus areas adapt the topics and learning support described in Erudite’s public programme information; confirm the current syllabus and requirements for your test cycle.

01

Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation

Consolidate mathematical basics and practise organising data, estimating, comparing options and solving chart-based questions.

02

Logical Reasoning

Build structured ways to identify relationships, test assumptions and work through analytical, deductive and critical-reasoning problems.

03

Language Comprehension

Practise vocabulary, comprehension, language accuracy and selecting answers supported by the passage or prompt.

04

General Awareness & Innovation

Develop a routine for static and current affairs across business, finance, economics, politics and sports, alongside innovation and entrepreneurship themes.
