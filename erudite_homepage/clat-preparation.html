COURSE OVERVIEW

Know what to work on.

For candidates considering undergraduate law programmes that use CLAT or related law entrance tests. These focus areas adapt the topics and learning support described in Erudite’s public programme information; confirm the current syllabus and requirements for your test cycle.

01

English language

Practise reading for central ideas, supporting detail, inference, vocabulary in context and the structure of an argument.

02

Current affairs & general knowledge

Build a consistent reading habit and connect events to relevant social, political and legal context.

03

Legal reasoning

Read a principle carefully, separate it from the facts and apply it consistently without relying only on prior legal knowledge.

04

Logical reasoning & quantitative techniques

Practise evaluating arguments, recognising assumptions and interpreting basic numerical information accurately.
