LEARNING IN REAL LIFE

Make daily practice small enough to repeat.

A short question can help build a routine. The local question on this page is an illustrative sample, not a live question feed.

QUANTITATIVE APTITUDEILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE / 01

A price is increased by 20% and then reduced by 20%. What is the net change from the original price?

Assume the original price is ₹100. Select one answer, then check your reasoning.

THINK IT THROUGH

A percentage increase and a percentage decrease are applied to different bases. Work out the new value after each step, then compare it to the original.

Practice noteAlways track the base value for every percentage change.
