HOW TO USE THE RESULT

Use the band to decide what to do next.

01

Look for a trend

Compare several mocks taken under similar conditions rather than relying on one score.

02

Inspect section floors

A strong overall mock can hide a weak section. Review accuracy and time use as well.

03

Check current criteria

Institutions update eligibility and shortlisting rules. Use the current official admission pages.

04

Update your plan

Use errors and missed questions to choose the next set of practice.
