Erudite’s public GRE programme describes classroom concept building, topic-wise questions, vocabulary support, personalised result discussion, writing feedback and full-length practice tests.

COURSE OVERVIEW

Know what to work on.

For applicants to graduate programmes that accept GRE scores. These focus areas adapt the topics and learning support described in Erudite’s public programme information; confirm the current syllabus and requirements for your test cycle.

01

Verbal Reasoning

Practise reading comprehension, text completion and sentence equivalence, with attention to meaning, evidence and precision.

02

Quantitative Reasoning

Revisit fundamentals and practise problem solving and data-related questions with a focus on clear steps and accuracy.

03

Analytical Writing

Learn to form a defensible position, organise a response, support claims and review writing for logic and clarity.

04

Vocabulary and test strategy

Use a planned approach to vocabulary building, timing, review and choosing which areas to revisit before the exam.
