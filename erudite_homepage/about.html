ABOUT ERUDITE / EST. 1998

Education that begins with the student.

Erudite describes itself as a competitive-examination preparation institution founded in 1998, with a student-first approach, personalised mentoring and learning support across classroom and online formats.

LEARNING IN REAL LIFE

Teaching begins with paying attention.

Erudite’s public positioning emphasises a student-first approach, personal guidance and preparation across multiple entrance pathways.

THE INSTITUTE

A bridge between aspirations and the next step.

Erudite’s official About Us page describes its work across MBA entrance examinations including CAT, XAT, IIFT, SNAP, NMAT, GMAT, CMAT and MAT, along with a Recruitment Training Programme for engineering students.

The institute says it focuses not only on teaching, but also on mentoring students and supporting their development. Its stated approach includes classroom training, counselling, e-learning modules, concept videos, regular doubt-clearing sessions and mock tests.

OUR VISION

Make quality education and guidance easier to reach.

Erudite’s published vision is centred on mentoring students and helping them translate aspirations into practical goals.

OUR MISSION

To support career-building through dynamic classroom training, personalised counselling and e-learning resources that respond to students’ goals.
