UNDERSTANDPRACTISEREVIEW

01 / MAT PREPARATION

Build speed without skipping the reasoning.

The MAT programme description focuses on strengthening fundamentals, practising real-style questions, reviewing performance and developing time-management strategies.

COURSE OVERVIEW

Know what to work on.

For management applicants using MAT as one of their entrance-test routes. These focus areas adapt the topics and learning support described in Erudite’s public programme information; confirm the current syllabus and requirements for your test cycle.

01

Language Comprehension

Review grammar, vocabulary, sentence completion and reading comprehension, with practice for choosing the clearest and most accurate answer.

02

Mathematical Skills

Revisit the foundations, then practise calculations, ratios, arithmetic and quantitative problem-solving with a focus on speed and accuracy.

03

Data Analysis & Sufficiency

Read tables, charts and combined graphs; learn to structure data and decide which information is sufficient.

04

Reasoning & General Awareness

Develop analytical reasoning and keep up with business, finance, politics, economics, sports and current affairs.

HOW LEARNING IS ORGANISED

Learn. Practise. Reflect.

Preparation is easier to manage when each session has a job: understand a concept, try it, review the result, and choose the next step.

01

Refresh the fundamentals

Special sessions and solved examples help consolidate core concepts before timed practice.

02

Practise and compare

Erudite’s published MAT course materials describe section tests, mock tests, actual MAT questions and comparative performance analysis.

03

Prepare beyond the test

The programme description also includes spoken-English support, institute-selection guidance and group-discussion/interview preparation.
